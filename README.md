# Vix Personal Video Creator By BK Han

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-v0.1%20draft-orange)

**Personal videos with your own face and your own voice, powered by HeyGen.**

## What it does

Vix creates HeyGen Video Agent personal videos that use **your own face** from a photo, **your own character**, and **your own voice** from a cleaned recording. It comes with three ready-made variations:

- **Hook**: a bold claim that grabs attention
- **Founder story**: your journey, turning point, and lesson
- **Offer**: your product with one clear benefit

Every video is reviewed before posting.

## Who it's for

- Coach BK Han's students
- Solopreneurs who want to show up on video without filming every time

## How it works

1. **Connect HeyGen**, then check your credits.
2. **Photo**: take a sharp, front-facing headshot of yourself.
3. **Voice**: record 1–2 minutes and clean the audio.
4. **Build once**: create your photo avatar and voice clone, then reuse them.
5. **Prompt the Video Agent**: choose a hook, founder-story, or offer variation.
6. **Review before posting**: check everything, then post only after approval.
